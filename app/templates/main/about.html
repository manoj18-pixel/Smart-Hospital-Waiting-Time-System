About MediQueue

Care that starts with respect for time.

MediQueue is a smart hospital appointment platform built around one principle: a patient's time is as valuable as a doctor's. We combine transparent scheduling with database-level slot locking and honest waiting-time estimates.

Our mission

Remove the uncertainty from hospital visits by making availability visible, bookings instant and waiting times predictable.

Our promise

One doctor, one patient, one time slot. No double bookings, no silent cancellations, no surprise delays.

Our standards

Secure authentication, encrypted credentials, role-based access and an auditable trail for every booking action.

How the platform works

Built like real hospital software

Patients book from a responsive website. Doctors manage their own queue. Administrators manage departments, doctors and schedules. Every slot is a database row with a unique constraint on doctor, date and time — the strongest possible guarantee against double booking.
